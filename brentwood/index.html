Brentwood Growth SMS Terms & Conditions

Brentwood Growth Corporation · Last updated October 8, 2026

1. Program description

Brentwood Growth Corporation ("Brentwood Growth", "we", "us") sends text messages to business owners who request a confidential Business Value & Transition Call through our lead forms on Facebook and Instagram and agree to receive texts. Messages cover a link to book your call, booking confirmations, appointment reminders and rescheduling notices about your request. We do not send marketing or promotional messages on this number.

2. How you opt in

You opt in by checking the optional, unchecked text message consent box on our lead form and submitting it. The consent language shown on the form is:

I consent to receive non-marketing text messages from Brentwood Growth about my call request, such as booking links, appointment confirmations and reminders. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Text HELP for assistance. Reply STOP to opt out. SMS Terms: https://terms.slategrowthpartners.com/brentwood/ Privacy Policy: https://www.brentwood-growth.com/privacy-policy/

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies, typically 1 to 6 messages per request.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

5. How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed. Reply START to resubscribe.

6. Help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or contact us at jbartlett@brentwoodgrowth.com or +1 833-631-6937.

7. Carrier disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Service is available on major US carriers to US mobile numbers.

8. Eligibility

You must be 18 or older and the account holder of the mobile number, or have the account holder's permission.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or your text message opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories of information described in our Privacy Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf. Full privacy policy: https://www.brentwood-growth.com/privacy-policy/
