1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy explains how we collect, use, protect and share information when you request a confidential Business Value & Transition Call and choose to receive text messages from us. Our SMS Terms & Conditions are at https://terms.slategrowthpartners.com/iei/.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you enter on our online request form: first and last name, email address, mobile phone number and company name.
  • Your text message consent: whether you checked the optional consent box, the date and time, and the form you used.
  • Records of the text messages we send you and any replies you send, including STOP and HELP requests.

3. How we collect it

You give us this information when you submit our online request form. Text message consent is collected only through an optional checkbox that is unchecked by default. You can submit the form without checking it, and consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

4. How we use it

  • To send the text messages you asked for: a link to book your call, booking confirmations, appointment reminders and rescheduling notices.
  • To answer HELP requests and to honor STOP requests.
  • To keep a record of your consent and opt-out choices.

5. No sharing of mobile information

We do not sell, rent, release or trade your mobile phone number or personal information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. All other categories of information described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent.

The only parties that handle your mobile number are the service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf (our messaging platform and the wireless carriers). They may use it only to deliver our messages.

6. Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. We will send one confirmation and then no further messages. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at the details below. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

7. How we protect it

Your information is stored in access-controlled systems, available only to the people who need it to respond to your request, and sent over encrypted connections.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your contact details and consent records while you remain opted in and for as long as needed to show consent and honor opt-outs. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing us; we keep only a record of your opt-out so we do not text you again.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
